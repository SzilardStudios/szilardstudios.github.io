Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: Road Universe Radio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or microphone. It only needs the internet to stream music, and the server that delivers the music can see the technical details every website or app server sees.

Who we are

Road Universe Radio ("the app") is made by Szilard Studios, an independent music project run by Szilard in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of data protection law (UK GDPR), Szilard Studios is the controller of any personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at szilardstudios@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app is a radio station for the Road Universe music catalogue. It downloads a song list and streams the songs from our own file storage, shuffled, and keeps them playing in the background with lock-screen and headphone controls.

What we collect

Information you give us: none. The app has no sign-up, no profile and no forms. Your favourite songs, if you choose any, stay on your phone (see below).

Information the app sends when it works: to play music, the app contacts our file storage to fetch the song list and the audio files. While music is playing, it also sends a very small request every few seconds to check that the connection is still working, so it can resume if you go through a tunnel or lose signal. Like any internet connection, these requests reveal to the server:

  • your IP address,
  • the time of the request and which files were requested,
  • basic technical details such as your device's operating system and the app version.

Our file storage and delivery is provided by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare R2). Cloudflare keeps ordinary server logs for delivery, security and abuse protection, under its own privacy policy. We may look at overall statistics (for example, how many requests the music files receive). We do not use this to identify, profile or follow individual listeners.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use advertising, advertising IDs or analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or share personal data.
  • It does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.
  • It does not send anything about you off your phone. If you mark songs as favourites with the heart button, that list of song IDs is saved on your phone only, so the Favourites station can find it. We never receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app. Your phone's operating system may also keep temporary audio data while a song plays.

Permissions

  • Internet access is needed to stream the music.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) are used only to show the playback controls (title, band, Play/Pause, Next) on your lock screen and notification shade. We do not send marketing or promotional notifications.
  • Background audio lets the music keep playing when the screen is off or you switch to another app.

The app stores and testing services

If you download the app from Apple's App Store or Google Play, or test it through TestFlight or Google Play testing, Apple or Google collect information about downloads, crashes and use, under their own privacy policies. They may give us summarised, anonymous reports. We do not receive your name or contact details from them unless you contact us yourself.

How long we keep information

We do not keep any personal data ourselves. Server logs held by Cloudflare are kept for the period set out in Cloudflare's policy. Data may be processed on Cloudflare's servers in countries outside the UK, under Cloudflare's own safeguards for international transfers.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. Because the app does not keep personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to give or delete, but write to us and we will check and answer. If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding accounts or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: szilardstudios@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Road Universe Radio mobile app. The main website, szilardstudios.com, is separate and has its own use of cookies and website analytics.
